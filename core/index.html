Tectonica core ontology

Version 0.1. IRI https://w3id.org/tectonica/core, prefix tec:. License CC BY 4.0. Download: Turtle (latest), Turtle 0.1.

An ontology of parts for architecture and the built world. Physical elements form the base layer; rooms and other human definitions are abstractions laid over it; components, containers, material states and provenance are separate logical layers that refer to the same element identifiers.

48 classes, 68 properties, 15 named individuals. Aligned to BOT; IFC 4.3 class names are given per class.

Classes

Box tec:Box

A rigid container with a lid or open top.
Subclass of: tec:Container, tec:Furnishing
IFC: IfcFurniture PredefinedType USERDEFINED ObjectType BOX

Building tec:Building

A structure that provides shelter, located on a site and decomposed into storeys.
Subclass of: bot:Building, tec:SpatialElement
IFC: IfcBuilding

Built element tec:BuiltElement

A physical element that is part of the construction (IfcBuildingElement in IFC4).
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalElement
IFC: IfcBuiltElement

Component tec:Component

A module that realises a human concept (access control, operation, shading) as a part of a host element. Stored on the layer side and attached to the host by its GlobalId.
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalObject

Container tec:Container

An object that holds contents, related to them by tec:contains.
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalObject

Curb tec:Curb

A raised edge between a road surface and a sidewalk or verge. IFC 4.3 entity (IfcKerb); no IFC4 equivalent.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcKerb

Curtain tec:Curtain

A textile screen attached to a window that can be opened or closed to control light and view.
Subclass of: tec:Component

Door tec:Door

A built element that fills an opening and can be passed through when open.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcDoor

Dwelling tec:Dwelling

A home: a group of rooms used by one household, laid over a building.
Subclass of: tec:HumanAbstraction

Exterior space tec:ExteriorSpace

The open space of a site outside buildings. It is the 'outside' node of topology graphs.
Subclass of: tec:Space
IFC: IfcSpace PredefinedType EXTERNAL

Furnishing tec:Furnishing

A movable but placed physical element such as furniture or a mat (IfcFurnishingElement family).
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalElement
IFC: IfcFurniture

Generator tec:Generator

The program, with its version, that applied rules to a brief.
Subclass of: prov:SoftwareAgent

Handle tec:Handle

A grip used to operate a door, window or container.
Subclass of: tec:Component

Hinge tec:Hinge

A pivot joint on which a door, window or lid turns.
Subclass of: tec:Component

Human abstraction tec:HumanAbstraction

A human made definition laid over the physical base layer (rooms, dwellings, zoning). It refers to physical or spatial elements and never replaces them.

Item tec:Item

A portable object that can be held in a container, for example clothing or a book.
Subclass of: tec:PortableObject

Key tec:Key

A portable component that operates one or more locks.
Subclass of: tec:Component, tec:PortableObject

Layer file tec:LayerFile

A JSON-LD document holding the non geometric layers of a model, linked to an IFC file by GlobalIds.
Subclass of: prov:Entity

Lock tec:Lock

A mechanism that keeps its host (usually a door) shut until operated with the key it requires.
Subclass of: tec:Component

Mat tec:Mat

A flat textile or fibre object lying on a floor or the ground, such as a doormat. Objects can be placed under it.
Subclass of: tec:Furnishing
IFC: IfcFurniture PredefinedType USERDEFINED ObjectType MAT

Material tec:Material

A substance elements are made of, with base matter properties: stability, decay rate and resistance.
IFC: IfcMaterial

Material state tec:MaterialState

The current condition of the matter of one element: its material and present stability, decay and resistance.

Norm kind tec:NormKind

Where a norm comes from: a design convention, a building code, a technical standard or an accessibility standard.

Opening tec:Opening

A void cut through a host element, placed parametrically on the host. It is not matter; a door or window may fill it, or it may stay open as a passage.
Subclass of: bot:Element
IFC: IfcOpeningElement

Outdoor area tec:OutdoorArea

A named outdoor place, such as a front yard, laid over an exterior space.
Subclass of: tec:HumanAbstraction

Physical element tec:PhysicalElement

A fixed physical part of the built world with exact parametric geometry, stored on the IFC side.
Subclass of: bot:Element, tec:PhysicalObject
IFC: IfcElement

Physical object tec:PhysicalObject

Anything made of matter: fixed elements of the built world and portable objects.

Portable object tec:PortableObject

A physical object that is carried rather than placed, with no own geometry at the current level of detail; stored on the layer side only.
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalObject

Provenance record tec:ProvenanceRecord

The record of one generation step: which rule, with which inputs and which generator, produced an element or record.
Subclass of: prov:Activity

Railing tec:Railing

A guard or handrail along an edge, such as a stairwell or a stair flight.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcRailing

Relative position tec:RelativePosition

How an object sits relative to the thing it is placed in or on. Instances: tec:On, tec:Under, tec:Inside, tec:Beside.

Road surface tec:RoadSurface

The physical paved surface of a road or path: length, width, elevation, material, curvature. Lanes and traffic are a separate movement layer. IFC 4.3 entity; no direct IFC4 equivalent (IfcSlab is used there).
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcPavement

Roof tec:Roof

The covering of the top of a building, usually an aggregate of roof slabs.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcRoof

Roof slab tec:RoofSlab

A slab that forms a plane of a roof; part of a tec:Roof.
Subclass of: tec:Slab
IFC: IfcSlab PredefinedType ROOF

Room tec:Room

A named place with a function, laid over a space.
Subclass of: tec:HumanAbstraction

Room function tec:RoomFunction

What a room is used for. Instances are named individuals such as tec:Kitchen.

Rule tec:Rule

A named, versioned, deterministic piece of generator logic.
Subclass of: prov:Plan

Site tec:Site

A piece of land with its built and unbuilt parts; the root of the spatial structure of a project.
Subclass of: bot:Site, tec:SpatialElement
IFC: IfcSite

Slab tec:Slab

A planar, mostly horizontal element: floor, ceiling deck or base slab.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcSlab

Space tec:Space

A physical void volume bounded by elements. It carries no human meaning by itself: names and functions belong to a tec:Room laid over it.
Subclass of: bot:Space, tec:SpatialElement
IFC: IfcSpace

Spatial element tec:SpatialElement

A part of the world defined by its extent rather than by matter: site, building, storey or space.
Subclass of: bot:Zone
IFC: IfcSpatialElement

Stair tec:Stair

Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcStair

An uninterrupted run of steps, part of a stair.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcStairFlight

Storey tec:Storey

A horizontal level of a building with an elevation, decomposed into spaces.
Subclass of: bot:Storey, tec:SpatialElement
IFC: IfcBuildingStorey

Terrain tec:Terrain

The ground of a site as a physical element.
Subclass of: tec:PhysicalElement
IFC: IfcGeographicElement PredefinedType TERRAIN

Wall tec:Wall

A vertical element that bounds or subdivides spaces. Openings are placed on its plane by coordinates along and up the wall.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcWall

Wardrobe tec:Wardrobe

A tall cupboard for clothes.
Subclass of: tec:Container, tec:Furnishing
IFC: IfcFurniture PredefinedType USERDEFINED ObjectType WARDROBE

Window tec:Window

A built element that fills an opening and admits light and view but is not a passage.
Subclass of: tec:BuiltElement
IFC: IfcWindow
